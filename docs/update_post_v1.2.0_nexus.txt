[size=5][b]Palversation v1.2.0[/b][/size]

Sorry for the long silence! Palversation was my first mod, and while I was away I realized there was a lot I could have done better. So I rebuilt most of it to make it much simpler to install and use, and fixed a bunch of bugs along the way. Your Pals also react to more moments now.

The main change: the launcher now finds Palworld and sets everything up by itself. Just download it from [url=https://github.com/CarlosNahuelcoy/Palversation/releases]GitHub[/url], open it, press Connect Account and then Play. No more picking folders.

Your Pals' memories and settings carry over. Thanks to everyone who kept reporting issues while I was gone, and thanks for your patience!
