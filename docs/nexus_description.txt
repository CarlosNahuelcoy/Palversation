[size=5][b]Your Pals finally talk back.[/b][/size]

Palversation gives every Pal a voice of its own. Deploy one, type [b]!pal[/b] followed by anything in the chat, and it answers in character: with its own personality, its own moods, and a memory of everything you've been through together.

They don't just wait for you to talk, either. Your Pals speak up on their own when something happens: when you call them out of their sphere, when they're hungry, when it's freezing, in the middle of a fight, when they get knocked out, when they level up, or just during a quiet moment on the road.

[size=5][b]What it does[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Real conversations.[/b] Ask your Pal how it's doing, what it thinks of your base, or anything else. It answers as itself, not as an assistant.
[*][b]A personality for every Pal.[/b] Each one gets its own character the first time you meet: one might be proud and blunt, another shy and clingy. Two Pals of the same species are still different individuals.
[*][b]Memory that builds over time.[/b] Each Pal remembers your past conversations, and older ones turn into long-term memories instead of being forgotten.
[*][b]A bond that grows.[/b] Pals talk according to their real in-game trust: a new Pal is guarded, while one you've traveled with for ages speaks like family.
[*][b]Aware of the moment.[/b] Day or night, how hurt they are and how strong they've become all shape what they say.
[*][b]Gifts and sight.[/b] [b]!palgift[/b] lets your Pal give you something it found, and [b]!palook[/b] makes it describe what it sees around you.
[*][b]Your language, your rules.[/b] Replies follow the language and tone you set, and you can edit any Pal's personality and memories from the launcher.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3625223587]UE4SS Experimental (Palworld)[/url], which lets Palworld load mods like this one.
[*][b]The Palversation launcher[/b], a small program that gives your Pals their voice. It has to be open while you play (minimized is fine). Nexus doesn't allow programs, so it's on GitHub:
[size=4][b][url=https://github.com/CarlosNahuelcoy/Palversation/releases]>> Download the Palversation launcher <<[/url][/b][/size]

[size=5][b]How to get it working[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install UE4SS Experimental (Palworld).
[*]Download the launcher, extract it anywhere outside the Palworld folder and open [b]Palversation.exe[/b]. It finds your game and installs the mod by itself.
[*]Press [b]Connect Account[/b], then [b]Play[/b].
[*]In the game, deploy a Pal and type [b]!pal hello[/b] in the chat.
[/list]
The launcher's [b]Setup[/b] tab shows a green check for each part. If something is red, it tells you how to fix it.

[i]The file on this page is the mod by itself, for people who prefer installing it by hand. With the launcher you don't need it: copy the Palversation folder into ...\Palworld\Mods\NativeMods\UE4SS\Mods\ only if you want to.[/i]

[size=5][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Local models:[/b] choose "Custom (OpenAI-compatible)" in the launcher and enter your server address (e.g. http://localhost:11434 for Ollama). If replies time out, raise "Response timeout" in the Events tab.
[*][b]Antivirus warnings:[/b] the launcher is an unsigned Python program, which some antivirus tools flag by mistake. Its full source code is on GitHub. On the blue Windows screen, click "More info" > "Run anyway".
[*][b]My Pal doesn't answer:[/b] check the Setup tab, then try "Test Full Flow" in the launcher.
[*][b]Singleplayer only[/b] for now. Multiplayer and dedicated servers haven't been tested.
[/list]
